Electrification Intelligence is a standalone research project tracking how electrification reshapes energy, industry and materials.

Our central question: is electrification becoming the dominant architecture of the future energy and industrial system, and what does that imply for manufacturing and materials?

We connect system changes to technologies, components and material requirements. We distinguish validated evidence, candidate evidence, interpretation and hypotheses. Sector growth alone does not establish a materials opportunity.

This website publishes curated research articles. Existing English research is the canonical source; Chinese editions preserve the same numbers, measurement boundaries and uncertainty in natural research prose.

Read the [methodology](../methodology/index.md), or explore the [six research hypotheses](../thesis/index.md).

## Project and content route

The [research repository](https://github.com/rethinksci-gif/electrification-intelligence) holds research; the [website repository](https://github.com/rethinksci-gif/electrification-intelligence-site) maintains public pages. Develop learning questions into sprints, then feed editions and thesis reviews.
