Editions cite indicator codes, evidence identifiers and project stages compactly. This page explains them. Definitions follow the project's reviewed indicator catalogue. A definition being validated does not mean that every observation of it is.

## Evidence labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **V** / Validated | The project checked the stated source claim or calculation within its recorded scope. A checked company claim is still a company claim. |
| **C** / Candidate | Extracted but not yet reviewed. Extraction confidence is not evidence confidence. |
| Interpretation | The analyst's inference from evidence. |
| Hypothesis (H1–H6) | A proposition under test, with stated conditions that would change the view. See the [thesis tracker](../../thesis/index.md). |
| `EI-E…` | Identifier of a validated evidence record. Editions resolve them in their evidence appendix. |
| `EI-P4-E…` | Identifier of a candidate record from the Phase 4 pilot. |

Grouped identifiers, for example two records from one source, are not independent votes. See [Methodology](../index.md) for the full reading key.

## Dates

- **Research cutoff:** the last date the edition's evidence was assembled.
- **Observation period:** the period a number measures, such as 2024 or 2025-H2. It is usually earlier than the cutoff.
- **Source date:** when the publisher released or revised the source.
- **Snapshot date:** when the project captured the source copy it checked.

An edition dated October can rely mostly on 2024 observations; the edition says so explicitly.

## Project stages

- **Phase 3 baseline:** the validated, snapshot-backed set of observations that current editions use.
- **Phase 4 pilot:** an offline replay that re-extracted claims from source snapshots already captured for Phase 3, to test an evidence-acquisition workflow. It produced no findings newer than Phase 3. Its records stay candidates until human review, and re-extraction is not independent confirmation.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning on this site |
| --- | --- |
| Clean Growth Ratio (CGR, K03) | Annual increase in clean generation (renewables and nuclear) divided by the annual increase in the system supply requirement. Below 1, clean growth did not cover the extra supply. |
| Supply requirement | Gross domestic generation plus net imports, as reported. Not billed consumption or peak load. |
| Band-IC price | Eurostat's non-household electricity price for annual consumption of 500–2,000 MWh, excluding VAT and recoverable taxes. A reference, not a specific factory tariff. |
| Nameplate energy | The rated energy capacity of storage in MWh or GWh. Not usable energy, delivered power or annual throughput. |
| Useful heat | Heat actually delivered to the process. Comparing electric and gas heating requires matching useful heat, not fuel input. |
| COP | Coefficient of performance: useful heat delivered per unit of electricity, for a heat pump at stated temperatures. |
| Matched service | The same output, timing, reliability and location compared across technologies. Cost comparisons without it are inputs, not conclusions. |
| Materials chain | System change → technology → component → material → property requirement → opportunity or risk. |
