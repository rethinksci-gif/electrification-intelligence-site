## Suggested review cadence

Review upstream releases, definition changes and licenses monthly; pin a version or commit when citing. Review candidate signals fortnightly, synthesize learning and sprints monthly, and reassess theses quarterly. This is a recommendation; scheduled acquisition is not enabled.

For each update ask whether sources, values or boundaries changed and whether earlier conclusions need revision. Activity alone does not justify importing data or raising confidence.
