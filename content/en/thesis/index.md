These are Edition 001’s qualitative editorial judgments, not formal confidence revisions or probabilities. Last updated: 2026-10-07. Power comparisons principally end in 2024; selected adoption, storage and price observations cover 2025.

## H1 — Clean Power Displacement

**Current interpretation:** Regional displacement; global structural decline unproved

**Status / evidence direction:** Mixed: EU supports; global fossil growth contradicts near-term displacement

**Confidence category:** Low–medium for sustained global displacement

**Latest meaningful evidence:** EU fossil generation fell 8.37% in 2024; global clean share rose [EI-E000164, EI-E000118]

**Main uncertainty or contrary evidence:** Global fossil generation rose 1.69%; CGR remained 0.760 [EI-E000120, EI-E000121]

**What would change the view:** Consecutive comparable periods of clean growth covering supply growth, accompanied by absolute fossil decline

Last updated: 2026-10-07.

## H2 — Solar + Battery System

**Current interpretation:** Enabling deployment exists; the dispatchable-service mechanism is untested

**Status / evidence direction:** Supports inputs; unresolved system effect

**Confidence category:** Low–medium

**Latest meaningful evidence:** US 2025 commissioned operating cohort: 46.787 GWh nameplate; stationary-pack benchmark $70/kWh [EI-E000812, EI-E000822]

**Main uncertainty or contrary evidence:** No matched solar charging, peak discharge, gas marginal-hours or K08 service-cost comparison

**What would change the view:** Metered solar-to-storage dispatch and a matched-service comparison with gas

Last updated: 2026-10-07.

## H3 — End-Use Electrification

**Current interpretation:** Strengthening in passenger-car sales; broader substitution incomplete

**Status / evidence direction:** Positive in transport; unresolved across all end uses

**Confidence category:** Medium–high for car adoption; low–medium for broad fossil substitution

**Latest meaningful evidence:** EU sales comparison is candidate evidence; global 25% is a validated reported statistic [EI-P4-E010–011, EI-E000814]

**Main uncertainty or contrary evidence:** Sales are not fleet use; EU electricity share of final energy moved only from 23.09% to 23.45% [EI-E000605, EI-E000608]

**What would change the view:** Fleet use and direct-fossil consumption moving consistently with sales adoption

Last updated: 2026-10-07.

## H4 — Industrial Electrification

**Current interpretation:** A concrete implementation case; general economics and geography unproved

**Status / evidence direction:** Narrow support; economic effect unresolved

**Confidence category:** Very low–low for a demonstrated broad economic shift

**Latest meaningful evidence:** Rondo's company-reported operating case; lower EU electricity reference [EI-E000824, EI-E000612]

**Main uncertainty or contrary evidence:** No metered useful-output costs, utilization or matched gas comparator; the EU tariff is not the US site's tariff

**What would change the view:** Commercial operating records showing sustained matched-service cost advantage

Last updated: 2026-10-07.

## H5 — Grid and Infrastructure Bottlenecks

**Current interpretation:** Credible constraint watch; increasing dominance not established

**Status / evidence direction:** Supports bottleneck concern; trend unresolved

**Confidence category:** Low–medium

**Latest meaningful evidence:** IEA procurement-pressure context [EI-E000826]

**Main uncertainty or contrary evidence:** No aligned industrial-load connection or delivered-equipment cohorts; cannot rank constraints

**What would change the view:** Cause-coded connection delays and realized equipment delivery times across comparable cohorts

Last updated: 2026-10-07.

## H6 — Materials Consequences

**Current interpretation:** One concrete component/material link; broader strategic consequences unproved

**Status / evidence direction:** Supports a narrow material link

**Confidence category:** Low–medium

**Latest meaningful evidence:** Thermal-storage case identifies brick and wire [EI-E000827]

**Main uncertainty or contrary evidence:** No qualified grade, material intensity, specification change or substitution series

**What would change the view:** Component specifications, failure data and bills of materials establishing an actual change in requirements

Last updated: 2026-10-07.
