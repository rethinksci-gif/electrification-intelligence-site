### EI-001

On 2026-10-21 the European Commission announced that Commissioner for Energy and Housing Dan Jørgensen will travel to Budapest, Hungary, to meet national authorities, energy operators and other stakeholders on high energy prices and housing affordability and availability.

The Commissioner will discuss high energy prices, energy security and REPowerEU implementation with Minister of Economy and Energy István Kapitány, following exchanges in Bratislava earlier that week with all Energy Ministers of the Visegrád Group.

The Commissioner will visit a grids development project by MAVIR, the Hungarian Transmission System Operator; the announcement states that modernising the network, reinforcing cross-border interconnections and expanding grid capacity are central pillars of the proposed European Grids Package and key enablers to structurally lower energy prices across Europe.

As part of his Affordable Housing Tour, the Commissioner will visit the Diákváros project with Minister of Transport and Investment Dávid Vitézy and other Hungarian representatives; the planned major brownfield regeneration project in southern Budapest is intended to provide affordable accommodation for around 12,000 students.

The Commissioner will also discuss EU housing policy efforts and priorities with Minister Vitézy in a bilateral meeting, and will hold discussions with the Committees on European Affairs, Social Affairs, and Energy of the Hungary National Assembly focused on measures to address the current energy crisis.

### EI-002

PJM set a record peak hourly load of 162.6 gigawatts on 2026-07-02, 1.3% higher than the pre-2026 peak of 160.6 gigawatts on 2025-06-23.

ERCOT set a record peak hourly load of 91.1 gigawatts on 2026-07-22, 6.5% higher than the pre-2026 record of 85.5 gigawatts on 2024-08-20.

SWPP set a record peak hourly load of 58.0 gigawatts on 2026-07-27, 3.5% higher than the pre-2026 peak of 56.0 gigawatts on 2023-08-21.

Most of these peaks occurred during regional heat waves amid the hottest summer on record nationally, which drove up cooling demand.

CAISO reached 50.0 gigawatts on 2026-09-09, below its record high of 51.1 gigawatts reached on 2022-09-06.

[Original source](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=68305) · 2026-10-15
