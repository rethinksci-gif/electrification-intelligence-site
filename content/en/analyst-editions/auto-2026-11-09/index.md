## Verified developments

### EI-001

The U.S. Department of Energy issued an emergency order directing MISO, in coordination with Consumers Energy Company, to ensure the coal-fired J.H. Campbell Generating Plant in West Olive, Michigan, is available to operate and meet emergency needs while using economic dispatch to minimize costs.

The Campbell Plant was originally slated to shut down on 2025-05-31, 15 years before the end of its scheduled design life.

In 2025, more than 17 gigawatts of coal-fired electricity generation were saved from going offline.

A joint report from FERC, NERC and NERC's Regional Entities highlights how events during the Winter 2026 Arctic Cold Period, including Winter Storm Fern, demonstrated the need for dispatchable generation capacity from facilities like the Campbell Plant.

The order is in effect from 2026-11-15 through 2027-02-12.

[Original source](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-secretary-keeps-critical-coal-fired-generation-operational-ahead-winter-midwest) · 2026-11-07

### EI-002

UK International Climate Finance support has enabled over 526,000 hectares in Indonesia to be placed under sustainable management practices.

10 moorings have been installed as part of a new mooring system that lets boats anchor without damaging reefs.

UK support helped Indonesia establish its largest marine protected area with community co-management.

The Coral Triangle contains 76% of the world's reefs and produces 30% of the world's tuna.

More than 85% of reefs are threatened by overfishing and habitat destruction.

[Original source](https://www.gov.uk/government/case-studies/protecting-the-worlds-most-biodiverse-coral-reef-ecosystems) · 2026-11-04

### EI-003

The UK government approved Sea Link, a 2 GW undersea electricity transmission connection between Suffolk and Kent developed by National Grid.

Sea Link is a 2 GW electricity transmission connection between Suffolk and Kent, running primarily offshore.

The 2 GW link is described as capable of carrying enough electricity to power the equivalent of 2 million homes.

The government community benefits scheme will be delivered by National Grid with over £1 million each for community funds in Kent and Suffolk.

The government also approved Great North Road Solar and Biodiversity Park near Newark in Nottinghamshire, with a generating capacity of 800 MW, enough for the equivalent of around 400,000 homes a year according to the developer.

[Original source](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/major-undersea-power-cable-given-green-light) · 2026-10-30
